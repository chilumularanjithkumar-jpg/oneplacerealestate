About us

نبذة عنا

One Place Real Estate & Gen. Maint. is an Abu Dhabi company that looks after the whole life of a property under one roof — from Leasing & Management to Cleaning, Building Washing, Maintenance and Consultancy.

Owners, landlords and facility managers hire us for one service and keep us for the rest. Every contract comes with a named account manager, a fixed scope and monthly reporting.

Our Visionرؤيتنا

To be the one place property owners in the UAE trust for every need of their building, under one roof.

Our Missionمهمتنا

To lease, clean and maintain property accurately and on time, with clear reporting, so owners earn more and worry less.

Real estate first.
Every service after.We lease and manage your property, then look after the building too.
