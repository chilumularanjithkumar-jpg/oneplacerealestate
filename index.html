What we do

Every service. One point of contact.

Owners, landlords and facility managers hire us for one service and keep us for the rest. Every contract comes with a named account manager and monthly reporting.

01

Buildings Cleaning Services

Scheduled and one-off cleaning for towers, villas, offices and retail units, delivered by trained, uniformed crews.

  • Daily lobby, corridor and common-area cleaning
  • Building washing — facade, glass and high-rise exteriors
  • Deep cleaning for move-in and move-out
  • Car park, water tank and waste room cleaning
02

Buildings Maintenance

Planned preventive maintenance and fast reactive repairs that keep systems running and tenants comfortable.

  • HVAC and AC servicing
  • Electrical, plumbing and MEP works
  • Painting, carpentry and civil repairs
  • Annual maintenance contracts (AMC) with emergency call-out
03

Real Estate Lease & Management Services

We lease your units and manage them afterwards, so you get rent on time without handling tenants day to day.

  • Marketing, viewings and tenant screening
  • Tenancy contracts and Ejari registration
  • Rent collection, cheque tracking and renewals
  • Owner statements and service charge management
04

Real Estate Consultancy

Independent advice on buying, selling, letting and improving property, based on current market data.

  • Market and rental yield analysis
  • Investment and acquisition advice
  • Property valuation support
  • Building condition and asset improvement reviews
