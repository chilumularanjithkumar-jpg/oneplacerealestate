Questions الأسئلة الشائعة

Good to know

Daily cleaning or one-off?

Both. Choose a fixed daily or weekly schedule, or book one-off deep and move-out cleans.

Can cleaning be tied to my tenancies?

Yes. If we manage your property, move-out cleaning is arranged so the unit is ready for the next tenant.

Who checks the quality?

A supervisor checks the work on a regular schedule, and you can report any issue directly to your account manager.

Can I add building washing too?

Yes. Facade and window washing can be added under the same contract.
